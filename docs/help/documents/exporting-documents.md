---
title: Exporting documents
summary: Download an engagement's working papers or final reports as one ZIP file.
roles: [Platform Admin, Super Admin, Staff]
order: 3
related: [working-papers, final-reports-client-review]
---

1. Open the engagement and go to **Documents**.
2. Choose **Working papers** or **Final reports** (and, for working papers, a **Request class** if you only want one).
3. Click **Export ZIP**.

The ZIP is prepared in the background. When it's ready you get a notification (**Document export ready**) — click it to download the file.

> [!TIP]
> To download everything a client sent for one request, use **Download all** on the request's **Submissions** tab instead.
