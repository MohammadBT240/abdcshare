ABDC Share is where the firm and its clients work on engagements together. Staff raise requests for the documents an engagement needs, clients upload them, the team reviews them, and final reports go back to the client for approval — all in one place, with a full history.

It also holds the firm's **Library** of reports, research and training material, and the weekly **Partner reports**.

## Who uses it

Everyone has one role. What you see in the menu and what you can do depends on it.

| Role | Typically | What they do here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Staff** | Practitioners | Work on the engagements they are on: raise and follow up requests, review client files, upload working papers, use the Library. |
| **Super Admin** | Partners and managers | Everything staff do, plus create and run engagements, decide reviews, sign off, send final reports to clients, curate the Library. Some are also **Partners** or the **Principal Partner** for partner reports. |
| **Client** | Your client's contacts | See their own engagements, answer requests by uploading files, discuss them with the team, and approve final reports. |
| **Guest** | Invited contributors | Write and submit a partner report when invited. |
| **Platform Admin** | IT / administrators | Manage people, clients and the lists the portal uses; keep the system healthy. |

The [Who can do what](/help/who-can-do-what) table lists every permission, role by role.

## The main areas

- **Dashboard** — your starting page, with what needs your attention. See [Your dashboard](help:your-dashboard).
- **Engagements** — each client assignment, with its team, requests, documents and stage.
- **Requests** — every document request you can see, across engagements.
- **Reviews** and **Final reports** — work waiting for a decision.
- **Reports** — weekly partner reports.
- **Library** — the firm's shared reports, research and training material.
- **Admin** — people, clients and settings (administrators only).
- **Help** and **Settings** — this Help Center, and your own account and notification settings.
