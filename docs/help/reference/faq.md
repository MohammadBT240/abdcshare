## Your account

### How do I get an account?

You don't sign up yourself. Firm staff are added by a Platform Admin; people at a client are added as the client's portal contacts. Either way you receive an email with a temporary password, and you choose your own the first time you sign in. See [Signing in](help:signing-in).

### I forgot my password.

Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the link in the email (it works once, for about an hour). If the email doesn't come, an administrator can reset it for you.

### Can I change my email address, role or department?

Not yourself — they're managed by an administrator. You can change your name, photo, phone and addresses in **Settings**. See [Your profile and password](help:your-account).

### Can I stop the emails?

Mostly, yes. In **Settings → Notifications**, untick **Email** for anything you don't want by email. Account emails — invitations and password resets — are always sent. See [Notifications](help:notifications).

## Engagements and requests

### Why can't I see an engagement?

Staff see the engagements they're on the team of, and client contacts see the engagements they've been added to. Ask the engagement's Lead or a Super Admin to add you. See [Team and client contacts](help:engagement-team-and-contacts).

### Who can change a request's status?

Staff on the engagement's team, and Super Admins. The stage (*Not Started*, *Submitted*, *In Progress*, *Reviewed*) changes by itself as files arrive and are reviewed. See [How requests work](help:how-requests-work#stage-and-status).

### Can I delete a request?

A Super Admin can, but only while the client hasn't sent anything for it and no documents are linked to it. See [Managing requests](help:managing-requests).

### How is an engagement's progress worked out?

It's the share of its requests whose status is **Accepted** or **Closed**.

### Who gets reminded about due dates?

Every morning, the people assigned to a request are reminded when it's due within a day or overdue. If nobody is assigned, the engagement team is reminded.

### Can an engagement go back a stage?

Yes, by one step — Execution to Planning, Reporting to Execution, or Completed to Reporting. Archived is final. See [Stages and sign-off](help:engagement-stages-and-sign-off).

## For clients

### Where do I see what the firm needs from me?

Your dashboard's **Waiting on you** list shows the requests waiting for you. Open one, go to **Submissions** and click **Send response** to upload files. See [Responding to a request](help:responding-to-a-request).

### A file was returned. What now?

Read the team's reason on the file and upload a replacement — see [Files the team returned](help:returned-files).

### How many times can I ask for changes to a final report?

A report can go through up to three rounds. See [Reviewing a final report](help:reviewing-a-final-report).

## Library

### Why can't I download a Library item?

Either it's **view-only** (you can read it in the browser but not download it), or its virus check hasn't finished yet — downloads open once it has passed. See [Who can see an item, licences and view-only](help:library-access-and-licences).

### Something in the Library shouldn't be there.

Open the item and choose **⋯ More actions → Report a problem** — the curators are told straight away. See [Reporting a problem with an item](help:library-reporting).

## Partner reports

### Who sees my partner report?

You and the Principal Partner, who sees every submitted report. See [How partner reports work](help:partner-reports-overview).
