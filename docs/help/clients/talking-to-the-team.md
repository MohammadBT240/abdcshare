Every request has a **Discussion** tab shared by you and the firm's team on the engagement.

1. Open the request and go to **Discussion**.
2. Type in **Write a message…**. Use **Attach** to add files, or **Mention** to get a particular person's attention.
3. Click **Post**.

The team is notified straight away, and you're notified when they reply.

> [!NOTE]
> Files sent in a discussion are for conversation. To deliver a document the firm asked for, send it on the **Submissions** tab so it can be reviewed and counted. See [Responding to a request](help:responding-to-a-request).
